Invoices and vouchers

Arabic invoices and receipt or payment vouchers that print straight from Odoo, in the company's own design.

120+ invoices a month

Before and after

Before
  1. 01Open a Word or Excel template
  2. 02Copy items and prices from the order
  3. 03Write the amount in Arabic words by hand
  4. 04Print, then type it into the system
  5. ProblemAbout an hour per document, and the paper didn't always match what was in the system.
After
  1. 01Click Print on the order or journal entry
  2. 02Document generated in the company design
  3. 03Amount spelled out in Arabic automatically
  4. 04Already recorded in Odoo
  5. ResultEach invoice or voucher now takes 2 minutes instead of an hour.

What it does

  • Invoices print from the rental order in the company's design
  • Receipt (سند قبض) and payment (سند دفع) vouchers from any journal entry
  • Amounts in words with correct Arabic grammar for each currency
  • A5 landscape to match the printed stationery
  • Fixed a broken customer label that standard Odoo printed
