Rentory app

A rental management app for phone and desktop. It runs standalone on its own backend, or connects to Odoo 19 when a company already uses it.

Androidphones and tablets
iPhoneiOS

Before and after

Before
  1. 01Orders kept in a spreadsheet or a notebook
  2. 02Availability checked by asking around
  3. 03Pickup checklist on paper at the warehouse
  4. 04Late days counted by hand
  5. ProblemSmall rental shops can't justify a full ERP, and Odoo screens aren't made for the warehouse floor.
After
  1. 01Order created on a phone or a desktop
  2. 02Units offered only when they're free
  3. 03Barcode scan, checklist and signature at pickup
  4. 04Return checked unit by unit, late fee calculated
  5. 05Saved to its own server, or straight into Odoo
  6. ResultThe same rental workflow runs on any device, with or without Odoo.

What it does

  • Orders with sections, drag to reorder, and lines booked, picked up or returned one by one
  • Availability per serial, confirmed by the server so no camera is booked twice
  • Pickup checklist, documents held and customer signature
  • Return condition, notes and photos for each unit
  • Late fees counted automatically, adjustable or waived
  • Camera scanning on phones, USB scanner on desktop
  • In Odoo mode: invoices, payments and printed documents
  • English and Arabic, light and dark
