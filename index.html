Custom Odoo app · rental and assets

Rentory

Before
Odoo's Rental and Inventory apps couldn't track equipment we rented in from partners. Contracts and customer IDs were checked on paper, late days were counted by hand, and customer questions and orders got forgotten.
After
One app with two views: Rental for orders and Assetory for equipment. Every serial has a status, location and rental history. Availability is checked per unit, barcodes are scanned into orders, a checklist shows which contracts are signed and which ID was left as guarantee, deposits and late fees are calculated, and gear rented in from partners is tracked with its supplier. Each unit is linked to its fixed asset in Accounting, so cost and rental revenue sit side by side.
Order mistakes dropped from around 10 to 1 or 2, and critical incidents from about 5 a week to 1 or none. No customer request has been lost since it went live.

Invoices and vouchers

Before
Custom invoices and receipt or payment vouchers were put together by hand, about an hour each, separately from the accounting.
After
Arabic invoices print straight from the rental order in the company's design. Receipt and payment vouchers print from any journal entry, with the amount written out in words in correct Arabic grammar for each currency, on A5 paper to match the existing stationery.
Each invoice or voucher now takes 2 minutes instead of an hour.
Odoo Attendances add-on · HR

Attendance Shift Guard

Before
Employees posted their sign in and sign out times in a WhatsApp group. Someone had to read the messages and type the hours up.
After
Check in happens in Odoo and is verified with a GPS geofence around the workplace. Each employee has their own shift. Anyone on site who hasn't checked in, or is still checked in after their shift, gets a reminder every 30 minutes. Overtime is requested with a reason, approved by a manager, and written into attendance automatically.
Working hours now come from verified records, not chat messages.
