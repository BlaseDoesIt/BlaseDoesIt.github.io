Odoo Rentory

A rental and asset management app built inside Odoo 19 for a camera and media equipment rental house.

Launched on 1 August 2026

800+ units tracked by serial

Before and after

Before
  1. 01Customer asks on WhatsApp or by phone
  2. 02Order noted on paper or in a chat
  3. 03Availability checked on the shelf
  4. 04Contracts and IDs checked on paper
  5. 05Late days counted by hand
  6. ProblemOrders and questions got forgotten, and gear rented in from partners wasn't tracked at all.
After
  1. 01Order created in Rentory
  2. 02Availability checked per serial
  3. 03Barcode scanned at pickup
  4. 04Checklist: contract, receipt, ID held
  5. 05Return check and late fee calculated
  6. 06Invoice created in Odoo
  7. ResultMistakes went from around 10 to 1 or 2, and no customer request has been lost since launch.

What it does

  • Two views in one app: Rental for orders, Assetory for equipment
  • Status, location, rental history and revenue for every serial
  • Equipment rented in from partners, tracked with its supplier
  • Deposits held apart from revenue, late fees calculated automatically
  • Booking planning on a timeline
  • Each unit linked to its fixed asset record in Odoo
  • Photos and files at pickup and return
  • Parts, maintenance and retirement of units
